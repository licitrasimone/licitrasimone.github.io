01. About Me

I'm a Red Team Specialist, penetration tester, and AI/LLM security researcher focused on offensive security and realistic adversary simulation.

Currently working at OPLIUM in Turin, Italy, I help organizations identify vulnerabilities, validate security controls, and strengthen their security posture through practical, evidence-driven assessments.

With a Master's degree in Computer Engineering from Politecnico di Torino and certifications including OSCP, CRTO, CRTL, and CPTS, I combine engineering depth with hands-on offensive security experience.

My thesis, "Leveraging AI Techniques for Automated Security Incident Response" (SOCAI Project), reflects my interest in security automation, SOC efficiency, and AI applied to real defensive workflows.
